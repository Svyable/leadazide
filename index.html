05 / Methodology

What this dashboard can and cannot establish

Evidence policy

Agency announcements are summarized and linked to their original publications. Reported sample counts describe agency activity, not the distribution or severity of contamination. This static snapshot is not automatically refreshed and does not independently verify laboratory results. The regional diagram is illustrative, not suitable for navigation, exposure assessment or emergency decisions.

Correction and update policy

New records should retain the publication date, source URL, data units and limitations. Do not overwrite an older advisory without retaining its provenance. Report corrections through the repository issue tracker.
